# ASTRAEUS Privacy Statement

_Last updated: 2026-09-17_

ASTRAEUS is a technical assistance agent designed to support research, software engineering, debugging, architecture analysis, system investigation, and technical documentation workflows.

## Data processing

Information provided to ASTRAEUS may be processed by the hosting platform and any connected services that the user chooses to enable in order to generate responses, analyze technical systems, and perform requested tasks.

Users should avoid providing secrets, passwords, private keys, access tokens, unnecessary personal information, or other sensitive data unless it is required for an authorized task and appropriate safeguards are in place.

## Connected services

ASTRAEUS may use knowledge sources, repositories, tools, APIs, databases, browser automation, or other external services configured by the user or administrator. Those services may have their own privacy and data-handling terms. Users are responsible for reviewing the privacy implications of connected services before use.

## Data collection by the creator

The creator of ASTRAEUS does not intentionally collect or maintain a separate database of user conversations or personal information through this repository. Data handling performed by Microsoft 365 Copilot, GitHub, or other connected platforms is governed by the policies and configuration of those services.

## Security

ASTRAEUS is designed to favor least-privilege access, read-only investigation where practical, evidence preservation, and explicit human approval for high-impact actions.

Users remain responsible for ensuring that their use of ASTRAEUS complies with applicable organizational policies, contractual obligations, security requirements, and law.

## Changes

This privacy statement may be updated as ASTRAEUS evolves. The current version will be maintained in this repository.

## Contact

For questions about this project, use the repository's GitHub issue tracker or contact the repository owner through GitHub.
